Rapid Rafting Adventures

Our purpose is to deliver unforgettable white water adventures that connect people with nature. Our mission is simple: safety first, fun always. Our motto — "Ride the Rapids, Live the Adventure!"

History

Founded in 1995 by a small group of river guides, Rapid Rafting Adventures started with just two rafts and a big dream. Today we operate five rivers across the region and have guided thousands of guests safely down some of the most exciting rapids in the country.
